“If your morality only exists when it costs you nothing, then how much is it really worth?” (A.J). Everyone strives to have a high moral standard, it’s part of being a human, and of a society; however, I believe the nature of humanity is destined to be evil and selfish. Do I believe myself to be different from a wretched man just because I say I am? What is the differentiation of morality between any one person? Most will say it's how you look at the world and the people around you. I would agree, but how is it possible to be different when similar mistakes are made, or when nothing is done to stand out as a good person? Simply thoughts cannot make anyone a good person, so morality must have a deeper level than believing oneself is good.
