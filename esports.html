Esports

- Esports Achievements -

Esports has had such an impact on my life because of the connections I was able to make through it. Relative to most esports players I have not played video games for that long. I have however, been doing esports for a while. I started playing Overwatch freshman year at Hampton Dumont high school, and am still competing now.

I finished high school out at Newton Senior High, where I co-founded an esports program. My Junior year however, the program hadn't started yet, so I played for Marshalltown Community College that year.

I am now playing for Buena Vista University, where I hope to make an impact on the program.
